## 1. Overview & Statutory Hierarchy

This Source-of-Truth (SOT) Register establishes the definitive medical and regulatory reference values used throughout the MediConnect application (frontend validation, backend safety safeguards, database schemas, and documentation).

### Jurisdictional Precedence Rules:
1. **Primary Statutory Authority (India):**  
   - Drugs and Cosmetics Act, 1940 and Drugs and Cosmetics Rules, 1945 (*Schedule F, Part XII-B*).
   - Ministry of Health and Family Welfare (MoHFW) / Directorate General of Health Services (DGHS) / National Blood Transfusion Council (NBTC).
   - *National Standards for Blood Centres and Blood Transfusion Services* (2nd Edition, 2022).
   - *Transfusion Medicine Technical Manual* (DGHS, 3rd Edition, 2023).
2. **Global Reference & Comparative Baseline (WHO):**  
   - WHO *Testing of Donated Blood for Transfusion-Transmissible Infections* (2010).
   - WHO *Blood Donor Selection: Guidelines on Assessing Donor Suitability for Blood Donation* (2012).
   - *Note:* Under Non-Negotiable Rule 1, WHO global recommendations are used for comparative reference and international interoperability, but **never override** Indian statutory mandates.

## 3. Discrepancy & Conflict Resolution Notes

1. **Donation Interval Discrepancy:**
   - *Conflict:* Prior code applied an unstratified 90-day interval to all donors.
   - *Statutory Truth:* DGHS Standards (2022) Section 3.3 explicitly mandates: **Male whole blood interval = 90 days**; **Female whole blood interval = 120 days**.
   - *Resolution:* Field `safetySafeguards.validateDonationInterval` and `DonorAssessmentPage.jsx` must accept gender and enforce the 120-day interval for females.

2. **Demographic Prevalence Discrepancy:**
   - *Conflict:* `bloodGroupsData.js` displayed Caucasian population statistics (O+ 38%, B+ 9%-20%).
   - *Statutory Truth:* Indian multi-center surveys published by DGHS/NBTC show **B+ is ~32%–35%** (highest in northern and western India) and **O+ is ~30%–34%** (highest in southern India). Rh-negative is low (~4%–5%).
   - *Resolution:* Update frontend dataset with explicit citation to Indian national serological surveys.

3. **Compatibility Conflation Discrepancy:**
   - *Conflict:* `canDonateTo` and `canReceiveFrom` conflated red cells and plasma.
   - *Statutory Truth:* Red cell compatibility is anti-A/anti-B recipient antibody dependent (O is universal RBC donor; AB is universal RBC recipient). Plasma compatibility is donor antibody dependent (AB is universal plasma donor; O is universal plasma recipient).
   - *Resolution:* Create distinct `compatibility.redCell` and `compatibility.plasma` objects in code.
